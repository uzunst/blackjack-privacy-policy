Privacy Policy – Blackjack

Effective Date: October 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Blackjack (the “App”), developed by Serhat Elmas, handles information when you use the App. The App is intended for an adult audience and uses virtual in-game credits for gameplay. These credits have no real-world monetary value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for real money.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name to play. The App stores certain gameplay information locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs, including your virtual in-game balance and game settings or preferences. This locally stored information is not transmitted to the developer’s own servers by the App.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on the services and settings used, Google AdMob and its SDK may collect or process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Advertising ID and other device or identifiers, where applicable;
  • App interactions, ad views, and related usage information;
  • Diagnostic, performance, and fraud-prevention information.

This information may be used for delivering and measuring ads, personalized advertising where permitted, analytics, fraud prevention, and service improvement. Google's processing is governed by its own policies and applicable settings. The developer does not control all data processing performed by Google.

4. How Information Is Used

The App uses locally stored information to maintain your virtual balance and remember your preferences. Third-party advertising services may process device and usage information for the purposes described above. The App does not operate a developer-managed player account system or server for storing game balances.

5. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell personal information collected directly by the developer. Google AdMob may collect, process, or share information as described in Google's policies and in connection with advertising delivery, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and related services.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored with PlayerPrefs remains on your device until it is changed or deleted. Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App will generally remove locally stored gameplay information, depending on your device and operating system. Information processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's applicable retention, deletion, and privacy practices.

7. Security

The App is designed to keep the gameplay information described in Section 2 on your device rather than transmitting it to the developer's own servers. Information processed by third-party services is subject to their security practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for an adult audience and is not designed to target children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the App. If you have a privacy concern, please contact the developer using the email address below.

9. Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can manage relevant advertising settings through your Android device and review Google's privacy controls. Requests relating to information processed by Google may need to be directed to Google or handled through its privacy tools.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App, its services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be available at the public URL linked from the App's Google Play listing.
